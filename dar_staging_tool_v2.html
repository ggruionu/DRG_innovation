👋 How to use this planner
1
Add a new job — tap + New Job at the top. Enter the house name, address, staging date, and expected destage date. Then tap the furniture pieces you plan to bring.
Pieces already in another job on those dates show in red — don't pick those.
2
Use the Load List tab before you load the trailer. It shows your next job's furniture organized by room. Tap each item as you put it on the trailer. Tori can hold the phone while you load.
Tap "Reset" when the job is done.
3
Use "What's Free?" when a realtor calls with a last-minute job. Pick any date to instantly see which pieces are available and which are already in a staged house.
No more mental math on the phone.
4
Mark jobs as Staged or Destaged using the buttons on each job card. This updates availability automatically.
Destaged = furniture is free again.
5
Print a job sheet or monthly calendar — each job card has a Print button. The Calendar tab has a Print Month button for a full paper calendar. Good for the van, the fridge, or the realtor.
Everything saves automatically in your browser — no login needed.
